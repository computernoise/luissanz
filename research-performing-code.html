Performing Code: Rhythm Generation and Structure in Computer Music

A series of solo laptop performances exploring algorithmic systems, digital synthesis, and rhythmic structures.

About

Rhythm generation and structure

In this series of solo laptop performances, Luis Sanz explores digital synthesis through algorithmic systems based on controlled randomness and integer-based sieve techniques. Developed in SuperCollider, the system combines evolving rules with real-time performer interaction to shape sound, musical structure, and rhythmic patterns.
