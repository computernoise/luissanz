Luis Sanz

Luis Sanz is a Zürich-based multidisciplinary artist, researcher, and musician working across computer music, experimental sonics, live coding, and audiovisual performance. His practice explores self-developed computational systems, autonomous sonic behaviours, rhythm, perception, and cultural memory.

He is part of unsorted in Zurich, a platform for experimental and computational practices. His work has been presented at Berlin Atonal, CTM Festival, Localhost Festival, Sonorities, A Bunch of Noise, and Proyecto AMIL, alongside touring and artistic research across Asia.

Projects and Collaborations

Selected projects and collaborations

Unsorted

Unsorted is an audiovisual collective dedicated to the exploration of open-source systems and digital sovereignty. Their practice focuses on the friction between technology and artistic expression, utilizing custom tools to develop new methodologies in computer music and graphics.

2025 –

Noijzu

Noijzu is a Swiss experimental unit formed by Miao Zhao and Luis Sanz. The duo focuses on acoustic sounds and computer-generated sonic entities.

2025

The Postscriptum Observatory

A listening space dedicated to what lingers after the fact. In collaboration with the Echoes of Authority festival, the project shifts focus toward the margins: notes, private reflections, and delayed voices that rework democratic life from below.

Mujica/Sanz

Peruvian-Swiss duo Mujica-Sanz explores Andean and Alpine sonic imaginaries by mixing global and local cultural elements through sound collages that resemble Peruvian and Swiss soundscapes, as soundtrack interventions through live music.

2021
Research

Selected research

Live Coding in Performative Contemporary Practices

An ongoing investigation into code as compositional material, a performative instrument, and a means of exploring emergent sonic structures.

Performing Code: Rhythm Generation and Structure in Computer Music

In this series of solo laptop performances, Luis Sanz explores digital synthesis through algorithmic systems based on controlled randomness and integer-based sieve techniques. Developed in SuperCollider, the system combines evolving rules with real-time performer interaction to shape sound, musical structure, and rhythmic patterns.

Synthesis For Unlocated Objects

Developed specifically for the exhibition space, this immersive sound installation focuses on the connections between sound objects, perception, and space.
