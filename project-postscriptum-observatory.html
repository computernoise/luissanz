The Postscriptum Observatory

The Postscriptum Observatory is a listening space dedicated to what lingers after the fact. In collaboration with the Echoes of Authority festival, this project shifts focus away from central statements and toward the margins: the notes, the private reflections, and the delayed voices that rework democratic life from below.
