About

Unsorted

unsorted is a Zurich-based collective run by a six-member core team: Alper Yagcioglu, Andrea Zaccuri, Ella Haechler, Luis Sanz, Paulina Zybinska, and Yann P. Martins. Working at the intersection of technology, artistic expression, and digital sovereignty, unsorted operates a studio in Zurich as a hub for research, collaboration, and knowledge exchange between artists and creative technologists. Its practice spans audiovisual performances, interactive installations, and workshops, with a focus on open-source technologies and custom-built tools for exploring new, “unsorted” forms of creative expression.

Alper Yagcioglu

Alper Yagcioglu is a multidisciplinary media artist and designer based in Zurich. His work spans graphic design, digital media, art, and tattooing, focusing on abstract drawings and graphic compositions that explore new and unexpected imagery. His experiments with tools and forms are playful and engage with media across video, sound, performance, live coding, and installation.

Andrea Zaccuri

Andrea Zaccuri works at the intersection of design and creative development, building digital projects for cultural and commercial clients. Interested in the web as a space for experimentation—and as a medium that can reshape how we interact, Andrea explores real-time images in the browser with unsorted.

Ella Haechler

Ella Haechler is a Zurich-based artist working at the intersection of technology and media art. Her live-coded music combines algorithmic composition and improvisation, using Sonic Pi to create soundscapes that move between ambient textures and electronic rhythms.

Luis Sanz

Luis Sanz is a Zürich-based multidisciplinary artist, researcher, and musician working across computer music, experimental sonics, live coding, and audiovisual performance. His practice explores self-developed computational systems, autonomous sonic behaviours, rhythm, perception, and cultural memory.

Paulina Zybinska

Paulina Zybinska is a creative technologist and lecturer who hacks her way through design, machine learning, and GitHub repos. She’s good at failing, but still making things run, proving that chaos is a valid coding strategy. In her free time she abuses TouchDesigner.

Yann P. Martins

Yann Martins is a debugger, researcher, and artist working within various media, including sound, noise, algorithms, browsers, photography, micro-controllers, 3D, game engines, and storytelling.
